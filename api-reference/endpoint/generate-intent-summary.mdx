<EndpointBar method="POST" path="/v1/generate-intent-summary" />

Generate the `intent_summary` of a Digital Human. If the intent is 500 characters or shorter, Bluejay clears `intent_summary` and the response has `skipped: true`. The response is 400 if the Digital Human has a workflow or voicemail tag, or has no intent.
