`init_tracing()` traces the Google Gen AI SDK. No extra package. `generate_content` and `generate_content_stream` are covered, including chats, Gemini, and Vertex AI.

```python
client.init_tracing()

reply = genai_client.models.generate_content(model="gemini-2.0-flash", contents=prompt)
```

Inside a [session](/sdk/overview#write-each-utterance), the span joins that call.
