`init_tracing()` traces Bedrock through boto3. No extra package. `converse` and `converse_stream` are covered.

```python
client.init_tracing()

reply = bedrock.converse(modelId="anthropic.claude-3-5-haiku-20241022-v1:0", messages=messages)
```

Inside a [session](/sdk/overview#write-each-utterance), the span joins that call.
