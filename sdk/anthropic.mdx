`init_tracing()` traces the Anthropic SDK. No extra package. Messages `create` and `stream` are covered.

```python
client.init_tracing()

reply = anthropic_client.messages.create(model="claude-sonnet-4-5", max_tokens=1024, messages=messages)
```

Inside a [session](/sdk/overview#write-each-utterance), the span joins that call.
